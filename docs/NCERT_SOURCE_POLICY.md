# NCERT source-use policy and permission verification

Canonical location: apps/lumeneios/docs/. Updated 2026-11-09. Scope: Mathematics and Science research, Classes I-XII. This reports source notices and unresolved uses; it is not a legal opinion or a permission grant.

## Evidence and interpretation
The official NCERT press release, Copyright Infringement in Educational Materials developed by NCERT, dated 7 April 2024, was directly inspected (PDF page 1): https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/announcement/notices/Press_Release_Copyright_Infringement-NCERT.pdf . It warns against unauthorised commercial publication of textbook content, in whole or part, and directs proposals concerning use of NCERT's name to its Publication Division / secy.ncert@nic.in. This is a verified contact route, not evidence that a request will be granted or that the Secretary is the sole approving authority.

The ePathshala Online Textbooks Section notice was previously discovered at https://epathshala.nic.in/wp-content/doc/book/btextbook/textbook.htm . Its indexed wording prohibits online textbooks in software/digital packages, redistribution and hosting, and requires written permission for links. Direct access remains unresolved in the current research; do not mislabel the indexed notice as directly verified. The April 2024 official release independently confirms that public availability does not establish commercial reproduction permission. No source-specific licence or written grant covering LumenEIOS has been verified.

Publication/reprint years, portal listings and download links are availability evidence only. Government hosting, an NCERT translation, a framework or an outcome publication does not automatically confer reusable software rights. A grant for one document/use does not extend to other publications or third-party components.
