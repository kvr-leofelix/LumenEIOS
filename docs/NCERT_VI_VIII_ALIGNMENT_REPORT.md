## Resource separation and interpretation

NCERT_VI_VIII_COVERAGE_MATRIX.csv contains 128 linked research records, not six title-only summaries. Records distinguish CURRICULUM_FRAMEWORK, SYLLABUS, LEARNING_OUTCOMES_DOCUMENT, PRESCRIBED_TEXTBOOK_WORK, TEXTBOOK_PART and LANGUAGE_EDITION. combination_id links all records for a class/subject. source_id links verified documents to the source register. Unknown documents have no invented source_id or official document URL; checked_publication_channel_url records where discovery was attempted. Resource/work IDs are research keys, not curriculum database IDs.

The separate syllabus and outcome matrices each have 36 records: six combinations x three languages x two explicitly distinguished historical/current contexts. Textbook editions have 24 rows: 22 listed parts and two explicit Urdu Part II gaps. A shared physical document may support several class records; these are coverage roles, not duplicate publications.

## Independent syllabus-language investigation

Directly inspected publication channels: https://ncert.nic.in/syllabus.php?ln=en and the linked elementary archive. The archive contains English Mathematics VI-VIII and Science VI-VIII documents. Hindi/Urdu filenames elsewhere in that archive identify language-subject syllabi; they are not evidence of Mathematics/Science translations. Separate English/Hindi/Urdu discovery searches were also performed; non-NCERT school/board syllabi were not substituted.

No applicable new-series class-specific syllabus was verified in any of the three languages. Hindi/Urdu versions of the historical subject syllabi were not identified in the inspected official channels. This does NOT establish that syllabi exist only in English, that no separate document was published, or that a document is officially unavailable. The unresolved states are NOT_FOUND_IN_CHECKED_NCERT_CHANNELS and publication/language status unconfirmed. They are not ACCESS_BLOCKED, UNAVAILABLE_CONFIRMED or NOT_SEPARATELY_PUBLISHED_CONFIRMED.

NCERT must confirm the current applicable syllabus publication/citation for each class/subject/language, or explicitly identify which source defines course content when no separate syllabus is published. A textbook foreword's framework reference is insufficient to make that substitution.

### Exact framework version caveat
The forewords identify the policy/framework designation NCF-SE 2023. They do not identify the particular retrieved framework print edition (whose colophon says October 2024). Broad reference edges therefore do not assert that this exact print edition was used to develop earlier textbooks or prove edition-specific conformity. The active matrix separates this from chapter-level alignment.
